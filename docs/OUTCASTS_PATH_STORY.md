# Down the Outcast's Path — Story Evaluation & Design Bible

Evaluation date: 2026-07-22 (Reed identity, party formation, erasure rules).

## Reed in one paragraph

**Marcus Reed** is Thyros's load-bearing private investigator — the person merchants, dock widows, and minor nobles hire when the watch will not file a report. He is not famous like Volo or Ophelia, but he knew where the bodies were buried, literally and figuratively. Years ago he took **Path coin** to bury a lesser case; when he tried to **resign** with a **ledger copy** tying consecrations to Volo's signature, **Lady Ophelia** bought his last meeting from **Whisper** and sent Path enforcers to kill him. **Magistrate Volo** signed the consecration that struck Reed's death from official ledgers. Reed matters because he sits at every faction's pressure point: Volo needs him forgotten, Ophelia needed him dead before he could trade the ledger, the Path needs him filed as maintenance, and the city needs him to stay a footnote. The party inherits **Reed's unfinished case** — his hidden proofs, his witness dossier, and the seventh-midnight binding that has not yet hardened against everyone who already read his name.

## Party formation

Reed did not die before sending work outward. In his final days he copied the ledger, hid pieces across the manor (notes, map pins, Calder letter), and compiled a **witness dossier** of people he trusted — compromised witnesses, outsiders, and anyone with an anchor stronger than civic fog. **Detective Jaro** was meant to deliver sealed summons; Reed died before every letter went out, but enough reached the gate that the party converges **the night they enter Thyros**.

| PC | Tie to Reed | Tie to the party |
|---|---|---|
| Investigator | Reed cleared a Path framing; carries his badge | Knows Jaro; was on Reed's short list |
| Ranger | Followed Path boots the night Reed died | May have seen another PC near the manor or cathedral that night |
| Cleric | Agnes trained them; Reed sent her patients whose names slipped | Parish overlap with the Paladin's buried report |
| Paladin | Buried a report for Volo — Reed was building the file that would expose it | Guilt mirror for the Investigator's debt |
| Wizard | Research blanks match Reed's rose notes (same editor) | Orlan at the tavern links wizard to dock and clergy threads |
| Barbarian | Cousin's prison questions overlap Reed's fourth map pin | Mira at the docks links to the sewer locket quest |

**Opening frame (City Gate):** *Jaro sent word — or you found Reed's seal on a letter you were already carrying. Either way, you are here because Reed thought you would finish what he could not. Meet at the Drowning Rat if you lose the trail.*

At the table, at least two PCs should share a prior thread (Jaro, Agnes, the night Reed died, or the witness dossier) so the group is not strangers doing parallel solo quests.

## Erasure rules

Forgetting is **three stacked systems**, not one magic button:

```
1. RECORD ERASURE (Volo's consecration orders)
   Ledgers, manifests, prison transfers, broadsheets

2. RITUAL BINDING (cathedral; completes at seventh midnight)
   Dampens civic memory — fog on the name

3. MAINTENANCE (Path enforcers, river accidents)
   Removes people who keep saying the name aloud

4. TITHE (the Lull Beneath)
   Names and memories consumed in the aquifer well — benevolent hunger
```

**What consecration does:** strikes or rewrites official documents; imposes soft recall penalty on anyone who only knew the victim through institutions (clerks, neighbors who "meant to ask"). It does **not** instantly delete everyone — it makes remembering **effortful and dangerous**.

**What the seventh-midnight ritual adds:** binds erasure into something self-reinforcing (bells, rose auras, blank folio pages). After completion, even strong memories start to slip unless anchored (personal objects, written notes, magical proof). TPK = the party is **unfiled**.

**Who still knows Reed (and why):**

| Who | Why they remember |
|---|---|
| The party | Arrived after Reed's death consecration but before the binding fully sets; named on the dossier or holding anchors (badge, letter, locket, guilt) |
| Whisper | Sold the meeting — guilt, not paperwork |
| Sister Agnes | Saw Ophelia's carriage; chose to remember via the hospice under the fourth pin |
| Jaro | Buried the Ashford file; operates outside Volo's direct ledger chain |
| Mira / Orlan | Personal loss or Calder research — anchors stronger than civic fog |
| Path elites | They do not forget; they **curate** |
| Random citizens | Surface only: "some investigator. Sad." |
| Gate guard | Knows the "river accident" script, not Reed's name |

**Rule of thumb:** if someone loved Reed, profited from his death, or was in the room — they remember. If they only knew him through the city's institutions, consecration got there first.

**Why the party is not erased yet:** Reed's death was rushed maintenance. The cathedral altar's blank **investigators** line is reserved for the party once they qualify as a threat. The seventh-midnight clock finishes the job on Reed **and** anyone still asking.

## History of the Ritual of Forgetting

The Path did not invent civic amnesia in Thyros. It **institutionalized** something older.

### Lineage (DM timeline)

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| **~50 years ago** | **Calder Bastion** purges the **Thorn Choir** — scholars who practiced the *Liturgy of the Unnamed*: rose-rites that struck names from records and living recall |
| **~40 years ago** | Choir survivors flee downriver; Thyros magistrates welcome their "mercy" during a dock riot and grain panic |
| **~25 years ago** | **Kester Volo** rises; reframes consecration as **civic stability** — disappearances become ledger hygiene, not cult crime |
| **~10 years ago** | Cathedral binding scaled from **surgical** (one name, one bell) to **batch** (whole rows, whole nights) |
| **Now** | Seventh-midnight cycle threatens **city-wide reinforcement** — Reed's rushed consecration is a preview |

### What the rite actually does

The cathedral ritual — the **Binding of the Unnamed** in Path liturgy — is not mind-wipe in one stroke. It is **editing**:

1. **Strike the record** — ledgers, manifests, marriage lines, prison rows (Volo signs; clerks obey).
2. **Dull the recall** — neighbors forget why they were angry; witnesses misplace the hour; hymns lose a verse.
3. **Anchor the edit** — bells, rose auras, and altar candles make the fog **self-healing** (scrubbed chalk re-fades; arguments re-split).

Path theologians call this **mercy**. Calder Bastion called it **heresy**. Thyros calls it **sleep**.

### Side effects (use at the table)

| Symptom | What players see | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Memory hole** | NPC pauses mid-sentence; cannot recall a name they knew an hour ago | Soft binding already touched them |
| **Story split** | Two honest witnesses disagree on headcount, hour, or who was present | One account was consecrated, one anchored by guilt |
| **Living unperson** | Someone still breathing, still working — but no one can quite name them | Surgical consecration without maintenance (failed kill or mercy edge case) |
| **Paper ghost** | Rent paid, meals delivered, cot warm — signature line blank | Records erased while body remains |
| **Chalk fight** | Names rewritten daily (Mira, dock board) | Civic memory resisting paperwork |
| **Blank verse** | Hymnal or folio with scraped stanza | Anti-liturgy eating sacred counter-magic |
| **Ledger gap** | Prisoner numbers skip (#46, blank, #48) | Batch consecration left a tooth gap |
| **Orphan drawing** | Child draws a man adults cannot identify | Personal anchor outlasting civic fog |

**Design rule:** every memory symptom should point back to **records → ritual → maintenance → tithe**. Show the wound before the knife.

## The Lull Beneath (eldritch truth)

The Path's politicians lie about **why** names must go. The ledgers are real. Volo's signatures are real. But the **hunger** underneath is older than any magistrate.

### What it is

**The Lull Beneath** — liturgical names: *She Who Keeps the Quiet*, *The Kind Hunger*, *Marah-of-the-Deep* — is a **benevolent-horrific** entity sleeping in the aquifer under Thyros. It is not a devil bargaining for souls. It is a **pressure sink** for collective memory: a thing that **eats names** so the city does not drown in everyone's lives at once.

| Public lie | Eldritch truth |
|---|---|
| "Civic consecration" | **Tithe** — names and memories fed to the Lull |
| "The Path maintains order" | Priests **keep the Kind Hunger sated** |
| "River accidents" | Sometimes **dessert** when the ledger tithe is not enough |
| Seventh midnight | **Great Feast** — city-wide binding when the Lull has been underfed |

**The bargain (~200 years ago):** Before Saint Elara's spires, Thyros suffered the **Bleeding Recall** — citizens woke remembering every ancestor, every stranger's childhood, every crime in the district **as their own**. The first cathedral was built to **cap a well** that drank those memories. Feed it **names**, receive **quiet**. Starve it, and the wells **sing back**.

The **Thorn Choir** at Calder Bastion refined the rite. The Outcast's Path inherited it. Volo believes — often sincerely — that he administers **mercy**. Ophelia believes she can **own the feeding schedule**.

### Why this fits Thyros

- **Noir:** The monster is not mustache-twirling evil. The city **chooses** forgetting because the alternative is madness in the streets.
- **Horror:** Memory erasure is not editing — it is **consumption**. Reed's ghost flickers because something **digested** part of him.
- **Moral knife:** Exposing Volo does not answer whether the Lull should be fed. Scorched-path may **wake a hungry god**; bargain-path **institutionalizes the tithe**.

### How NPCs talk about it (never dump at once)

| NPC | Knows | Says |
|---|---|---|
| **Volo** | Full bargain; frames as public health | "You think I enjoy this? I keep the wells quiet." |
| **Ophelia** | Feeding schedule; wants control | "The Quiet prefers rose incense. I prefer obedience." |
| **Sister Agnes** | Church made the first pact; guilt | "Saint Elara's mercy was always… a diet." |
| **Orlan** | Calder texts on Marah-of-the-Deep | "The Choir called it kind. That is what terrifies me." |
| **Reed (echo)** | Glimpsed the well; fragment only | "It thanked me. That was worse than the rose." |
| **Path adepts** | Liturgy only | "We feed the Kind Hunger so children can sleep." |

### Encounter ladder (expose the entity)

| Stage | Where | What happens | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **Wrong reflection** | Sewers — Listening Pool | Pool shows faces that are not yours; whisper of gratitude | `lull_glimpsed` |
| 2. **Name runoff** | Sewers — name-slips | Slips are **chewed** edges, not just wet | `sewer_name_slip_found` |
| 3. **Well grate hymn** | Docks — aquifer grate | Hum beneath dock; WIS/Religion vision — starving vs fed city | `lull_mercy_understood` |
| 4. **Cell thirteen offering** | Prison — cell 13 | Ash-rose bowl; memories pooled like oil | `cell13_offering_seen` |

| 6. **The Well Beneath** | Cathedral — crypt grate | Full glimpse: benevolent mass, patient hunger, seventh bell as dinner bell | `lull_well_seen` |

**Memory Shades** (`memory_shade` NPC): CR ~3 psychic remnants — not the Lull itself, but **shavings** of consumed identities. They whisper wrong names, fight until dispersed, leave oily memory-residue clues.

### Endings and the Lull

| Ending | Lull's fate | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| **Expose / default victory** | Tithe disrupted; city remembers; Lull stirs hungry — riots **and** rising madness | Pyrrhic truth |
| **Ophelia bargain** | Feeding continues under new management | Quiet, institutional horror |
| **Scorched path** | Altar broken; Lull **fully wakes** in the aquifer — no more curated forgetting, no more curated mercy | Vacuum + cosmic dread |
| **TPK** | Party becomes **dessert** | Names unmade |

## Verdict

**Strong noir premise, now playable.** Thyros uses memory-erasure as civic machinery, Ophelia's bargain as a real ending, and Reed's complicity as the midpoint knife. Cathedral access requires two investigation proofs. The Drowning Rat hosts Sister Agnes and the tavern witness. Victory narration variants resolve from session flags.

| Dimension | Status |
|---|---|
| Premise | Reed was Path-adjacent, tried to leave; Ophelia had him killed; Volo erased it |
| Antagonist shape | Power struggle: Ophelia uses the party to replace Volo |
| Clues | Confirm + twist (complicity, church, memory-erasure) via Interact checks |
| Urgency | Seventh midnight clock (bells count once per midnight after entry) |
| Ending | Pyrrhic default + `ophelia_bargain` / `scorched_path` variants |
| Moral choices | Expose, bargain with Ophelia, or burn the binding |

## Murder truth (canonical)

**Lady Ophelia** paid **Whisper** for Reed's last meeting and sent Path enforcers to kill him. **Magistrate Volo** signed the consecration that erased the death from ledgers.

Three fair trails:

1. Whisper confession (sold meeting to cathedral-silk / rose-pin woman)
2. Reed's notes margin: `O. knows I have the ledger copy — she smiled`
3. Sister Agnes: saw Ophelia's carriage near Reed's lane; her clinic was the fourth map pin

## Layered truth (DM / LLM bible)

**Surface (Act 1):** Marcus Reed was murdered. Thyros looks away.

**Working theory (Act 2):** The Outcast's Path — black rose in a thorn circle — operates from cathedral, prison, and sewers. Volo signs the orders.

**Knife twist (mid):** Reed took Path money, then tried to leave. Badge reverse shows a half-scratched Path mark.

**Dark twist (Act 3):** Ophelia arranged the hit and offers a bargain: remove Volo, leave the Path under her. Cathedral ritual **binds memory and records**.

**Eldritch truth (Act 4 / cathedral):** Forgetting is **tithe**. **The Lull Beneath** — the Kind Hunger in the aquifer — eats names so Thyros does not suffer the Bleeding Recall. Volo administers mercy; Ophelia wants the feeding schedule. The seventh midnight is a **Great Feast**.

**Endings:**

| Choice | How it wires | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Stop ritual + expose Path | Default / cathedral by_map victory | Truth + riots |
| Accept Ophelia's bargain | Session `victory_variant: ophelia_bargain` (she says "then we have a bargain") | Quiet, forgetful peace |
| Shatter the altar | Athletics check on altar → `ritual_scorched` | Vacuum of power |
| Fail / TPK | TPK narration | Party erased |
